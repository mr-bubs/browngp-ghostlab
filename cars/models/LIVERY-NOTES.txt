Mercedes reference livery recreation

The existing generic open-wheel model retains its original geometry and licence.
Its paint is a simplified recreation of the user-supplied Mercedes references:
silver nose, black body, turquoise trim, silver sidepod stripes, star pattern,
sponsor lettering and driver numbers 63 and 12. This is not an official Mercedes
model or an exact reproduction of the constructor's bodywork or sponsor artwork.

mercedes-livery.js creates model-space paint maps once per car. Painting is part
of each existing body material, so opacity, depth and cockpit stencil masking
continue to apply without separate overlapping decal objects. No camera,
telemetry, racing-line or lap-timing changes are included in this update.
