V4.4 is an authored real-time environment reconstruction, not a laser scan.
Buildings, water and woodland use the existing mapped footprints. Temporary
grandstands, race advertising, barriers, gantries and spectator placements are
approximations. It does not reproduce every detail of the 2026 event.
